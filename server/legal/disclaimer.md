## What a MirrorMirror report is

A MirrorMirror preview or report is a summary of what **free, publicly available sources** said about a business on the date we read them. The sources include company registries, Google Maps and reviews, the company's own websites, social media, directories and news. Parts are written or checked by AI.

It is **not** an audit, inspection, investigation, credit report, legal or financial due-diligence report, or professional advice of any kind.

## Limits you should know

1. **Public sources can be wrong, incomplete or out of date**, and can change after we read them. Every report is "as at" its date.
2. **"Not found" means we did not find it** in the sources we read. It does not mean it does not exist.
3. **We have not contacted the company**, visited its premises or seen private records.
4. **Warning signs are flags, not accusations.** A label such as "contradiction", "missing", "outdated" or "wrong" means public sources disagree, are silent, or look old. It is not a finding that anyone did something wrong.
5. **AI makes mistakes.** AI-written text can be wrong or miss context. What an AI assistant says about a company changes from one ask to the next. The "AI assistants" chapter is a **snapshot** of the answers at that time, not a promise of what customers will see later.
6. **Suggestions are best effort.** Fixes, plans and rewritten text are suggestions based on limited public information. We do not promise any rise in Google rank, reviews, sales or AI recommendations. We do not control Google, AI assistants, registers or reviewers.
7. **Do not rely on a report alone.** If you are checking a supplier or a rival, confirm what matters with the company or the official register before you sign, pay, hire, publish or decide.
