# Corrections and removal

Last updated: [DATE]

MirrorMirror.cloud makes reports about businesses from free public sources, with AI and on a best-effort basis. Sometimes a source is wrong, out of date, or we read it wrongly. If a report about your business, or about you, has something wrong, we want to fix it.

## Who can ask

- **The business** a report is about.
- **A person named** in a report (director, owner, staff member, reviewer).
- Anyone with a **legal claim** about the content (for example a copyright owner).

You do not need to have bought anything, and you do not need an account.

## What to send

Email [corrections@mirrormirror.cloud](mailto:corrections@mirrormirror.cloud) with:

1. the link to the preview or report (or the company name and country);
2. what is wrong, and what is correct;
3. any proof that helps (a register entry, your current website, a document);
4. how we can reach you.

If you want your **personal data** removed, tell us which name or detail. You do not have to give a reason.

## What we do

- We confirm we received your request within [2] working days, and aim to finish within [5] working days.
- If you are right, we **correct** the item, **mark** it with a note, or **remove** it. The rest of the report stays available.
- Personal data of a named person is removed on request unless we have a strong legal reason to keep it, which we will explain.
- We do not tell you who ordered a report, and we do not tell the person who ordered it who asked for a correction.
- If we disagree, we explain why, and you may add a short statement that we show next to the item.

## Not satisfied?

Write to hello@mirrormirror.cloud, or contact the data protection authority in your country (see our [Privacy Notice](/privacy)).
