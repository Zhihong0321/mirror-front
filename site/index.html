Questions, answered.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We research the company before you pay anything, and the preview shows you what we found. You decide after reading it.

Can I order a report on another company?

Yes. Anyone can order the report on any company: a competitor, a supplier, a business you plan to work with. It is the same report. We use public sources only, we leave out personal data, and we never tell the company who asked.

Where does your information come from?

Public sources only: business registers, Google Maps, directories, your own websites, social media, news and the answers AI assistants give. Every fact in the report links to where we found it.

Which AI assistants do you check?

We ask AI models the questions your customers ask, such as “who is the best installer in my town?”, both from memory and with web search, and record whether they name you, a rival, or wrong details about you.

How long does it take?

The research behind the preview takes minutes once it starts. The full report takes longer, because it checks every town you serve, every page of your sites and each claim in its register. You get one link when it is ready.

What if a section of my report finds little?

The report says so plainly. We never pad a chapter or promise a number we have not found.

Do you share what you find about my company?

No. Your preview and your report sit behind a long private link that search engines do not index. Personal data we find exposed online is a private warning to you, never bait.

Which countries do you cover?

Any country where your customers search Google and ask AI. Registry checks use each country’s public company and licence registers, so their depth depends on what that country publishes.
