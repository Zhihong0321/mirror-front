Questions, answered.

Do I have to apply?

No. Every account can refer. Sign in with your email and your referral link is ready.

Does it cost me anything?

No. Previews are free to ask for and free for the owner to read. You only earn; you never pay.

Is the company told who asked for its preview?

No. The company in a preview is never told who asked for it. You decide whether to send it, and to whom.

Can I ask for a preview of a competitor?

Yes. Anyone can get a preview of any company: their own, a competitor or a supplier. It is the same research.

How is the sale credited to me?

By the code in your link. If the owner comes back later, a cookie remembers your link for 30 days for that report. A preview you asked for counts as yours too.

What does the tracker show me?

When the link was opened, roughly where from (the city), on which device, how long the reader stayed and how far they scrolled. Never names, emails or IP addresses. Your own visits are not counted.

Which countries can I refer in?

Any. We have research prompts for 25 countries; Malaysia, Singapore, Australia and South Korea also get industry-specific research. Other countries get our general research.
