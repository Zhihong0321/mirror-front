# The business

## In one line

MirrorMirror tells a business what customers, search engines and AI assistants see when they look it
up, and hands it the fixes, ready to use.

## The name

From Snow White: *"Mirror, mirror on the wall, who's the fairest of them all?"* The magic mirror
always tells the truth, even when it hurts. Today the mirror people ask is AI: "who's the best solar
installer in Penang?" MirrorMirror shows whether that answer names you.

Tagline: **Mirror, mirror on the cloud — whose name does AI say out loud?**

Use the fairy tale freely (public domain). Avoid Disney's look: their Evil Queen, their mirror design,
their name.

## What we sell

An **online presence audit**, answering one question from three sides:

| Side | Question |
|---|---|
| How AI sees you | What AI assistants say about the company, and whether they recommend it in its trade and area |
| How the internet sees you | Whether its details agree across website, Google Maps, social media and directories, and where it ranks |
| How the public sees you | What customers say in reviews, and whether the company answers them |

### Chapters of the full report

| Chapter | The question | Number unlocked |
|---|---|---|
| Reputation | Do reviews still decide who gets the call? | Reviews with a reply from you |
| Search and Maps | When a customer searches, do they find you? | Your rank on Google Maps |
| AI assistants | How important is an AI recommendation in today's market? | AI answers that recommend you |
| Your websites | Does your website win the customer, or lose them? | Urgent faults on your sites |
| Licences and awards | Can a stranger verify what you claim? | Claims the registers confirm |

Plus the done-for-you work: a rewritten homepage, review replies, pages to add, an AI plan, business
data for Google and AI, and a 30-day plan. Delivered as one link: desktop, mobile and A4 (print / PDF).

## One report, any company, four reasons to buy (decided 2026-10-30)

There is one product: the report. **Anyone can buy the report on any company.** The buyer does not have
to be the company in it. So these are four reasons to buy the same report, not four products:

| Reason | Line | Who buys |
|---|---|---|
| Read it | Read it before your competitor does | The owner, about their own company |
| Fix it | Fix it before your customers see it | The owner, about their own company |
| Read your competitor | Read your competitor: an advantage nobody else has | A business, about a rival |
| Check a supplier | Looking for a supplier or vendor? Check them first | A buyer, about a supplier or vendor |

The fairy tale fits all four: the queen's mirror also told her who her rival was.

Rules this brings:
- When the buyer is not the company in the report, exposed personal data must stay out of what they see
  (today it is a private warning to the owner).
- Never tell the company in the report who bought it, and do not treat it as a lead because of the purchase.
- Part of the PDPA review below.

## The selling pipeline (decided 2026-10-30)

1. **How customers find us: email marketing.** We email the businesses on the lead list.
2. **The email gives them a link to their free report: the preview (`/dp/<public_id>`)**, written from
   their company dive. Not the raw company dive page (`/d/`).
3. **The preview tells them to buy the full report** (its locked findings and order panel).

## How it sells (the funnel)

0. **Find the prospects.** The country industry tool lists every business of one industry in one
   country (e.g. advertising agencies in Malaysia), with the emails on their websites.
1. **Research first.** We run a company dive on a prospect before they pay. It is cheap.
2. **Free preview.** The owner gets a landing page about their company and nobody else: what public
   sources say about them, and warning signs (contradictions, missing, outdated, wrong). A few are shown
   in full; the rest, and the five key numbers, are locked.
3. **Pay.** The owner orders the full report.
4. **Full report.** The paid pipeline runs on the same research and delivers the report.
5. **Later:** a monthly re-check ("your score went from 54 to 71").

Rules for the preview:
- Every number it shows has already been found. Never promise more than the report delivers.
- Exposed personal data (staff numbers, names) is a private warning, never used as bait.

## Who buys

- **Now:** business owners in Malaysia (SMEs, contractors, manufacturers, shops), auditing their own
  presence.
- **Next:** advertising and media agencies, first in Malaysia, then other countries. They sell the
  report to their clients, and later become resellers under their own brand.
- Each country gets its own pipeline (its registries, job sites, social media, legal rules, report
  language). Malaysia is first.
